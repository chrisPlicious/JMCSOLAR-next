From site visit to switch-on.

The same sequence on every job, whether it is a 6 kW roof in Cogon or a 100 kW farm shed. You always know which step you are on.

  1. Step 1

    Site assessment

    An engineer measures the roof, checks your panel board and reads your bills. You get a written proposal, not a guess.

  2. Step 2

    Design & permits

    String design, protection, and the DOE/ERC and net-metering paperwork with your electric cooperative.

  3. Step 3

    Installation

    Rail-mounted arrays, labelled trunking, proper breakers. Clean work you can show the next engineer.

  4. Step 4

    Commissioning

    Insulation and polarity tests, inverter configuration, monitoring app set up on your phone.

  5. Step 5

    Monitoring & O&M

    We keep watching the numbers. Cleaning, diagnostics and after-sales support when you need it.
