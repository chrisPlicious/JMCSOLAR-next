07:30 · what is in the drawing

Six parts. One quiet system.

  1. PV array

    Rail-mounted modules on the sun-facing slope. Sized to your roof and your bill, not to a package.

  2. Hybrid inverter

    Turns DC from the roof into AC for the house, decides what to store, and islands the home when the grid drops.

  3. LiFePO4 battery

    Stored daylight for the evening and for brownouts. Wall-mounted, monitored from your phone.

  4. Net meter

    Exports surplus to LEYECO and credits it on your bill. We file the DOE/ERC and cooperative paperwork.

  5. Solar pump

    For farms: a ground-mounted array running the irrigation pump at zero fuel cost.

  6. EV charger

    A dedicated circuit in the carport, fed by your own daytime solar.
